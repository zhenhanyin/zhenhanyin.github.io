About me

I am a master's student in Electronic Information at Tongji University. My research focuses on embodied AI and robot learning, especially vision-language-action models and structured representations of the physical world.

I am interested in how robots can use visual observations, language instructions, and 3D reasoning to make reliable decisions and act in changing environments.

Research interests

  • Embodied AI and robot foundation models
  • Vision-language-action learning
  • World-action models and 3D spatial reasoning
