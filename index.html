03 / ABOUT

A little more about me.

I am pursuing a master’s degree in Electronic Information at Tongji University. My research centers on embodied AI and robot foundation models, with a particular interest in how structured 3D world representations can support reliable action generation.

I enjoy working across model design, training, and evaluation, and turning research ideas into systems that can be tested on real robots.
